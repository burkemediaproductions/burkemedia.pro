About BurkeMedia

A Palm Desert creative and digital marketing studio with a practical small-business heart.

BurkeMedia Productions helps businesses tell their story through websites, video, photography, branding, social media, SEO, AI-assisted content and automation.

Local, creative, practical

Built around real business owners and real content.

We are local to the Coachella Valley, but our real specialty is making the process feel manageable for business owners who are busy running the business.

Instead of asking clients to invent perfect content from scratch, we interview, photograph, film, organize and build around what already makes the business valuable.
