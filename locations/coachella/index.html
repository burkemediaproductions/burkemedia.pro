Do you work directly with Coachella businesses?

Yes. BurkeMedia works with businesses and organizations in Coachella and across the Coachella Valley. We can help with a single website update, a larger rebuild, a video shoot, SEO/AIO content, social content or a connected system.

Yes. That is usually the best approach. A planned photo/video shoot can create homepage visuals, service page media, reels, Google Business updates, YouTube clips and future campaign content.

Do you only build websites?

No. Websites are often the center of the work, but BurkeMedia also helps with video, photography, branding, SEO/AIO, social media content, AI consulting, forms, lead tracking and practical automation.
