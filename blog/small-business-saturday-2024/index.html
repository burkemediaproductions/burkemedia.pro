Celebrate Small Business Saturday with BurkeMedia Productions: Supporting Local Businesses Like Ours!

A Small Business Saturday message from BurkeMedia Productions about supporting local businesses and helping them improve their websites, apps, branding, social media and video.

Small Business Saturday 2024 • By Michael Ray Burke

We know firsthand the challenges and triumphs of running a small business — why? Because we’re one too. Small businesses are the heart and soul of our communities, driving local economies and bringing people together — from small shops and local restaurants to skilled mechanics, handymen and home-based entrepreneurs. We have been helping small businesses for nearly 13 years. This is your time to shine, and we are here to help you thrive.

To show our appreciation for everything you do, we offered 5% off all our services for small businesses starting November 30 through Saturday, December 7. Whether a business needed a refreshed website with robust SEO, apps to communicate with customers or ease team workflows, help with social media, striking branding materials or dynamic video production, we were ready to partner with them to take the business to the next level.

How can BurkeMedia Productions help my business?

As a fellow small business, we are passionate about helping local entrepreneurs like you succeed. We specialize in creating solutions that connect you with your audience, grow your brand and help you stand out. Whether it is highlighting your menu, showcasing your services or sharing your unique story, we are here to make it happen.

This was a chance for small businesses to invest in their business and save while doing it. Together, we can create something amazing for the holiday season and beyond.

Even when a seasonal promotion ends, the message still matters: small businesses deserve websites, content, branding and systems that help them look professional and stay visible.
