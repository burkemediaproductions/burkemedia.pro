Revive Your Ride: Platinum Auto Repair and Collision New Digital Presence with Websites, Apps, Video, and New Social Media Presence

A look at the website, video, lead generation and social media support BurkeMedia built for Platinum Auto Repair & Collision.

BurkeMedia Blog • Featured Client

Platinum Auto Repair and Collision needed a stronger digital presence that could show the quality of its work, help customers request information and support ongoing social media visibility.

For an auto repair and collision business, trust matters. Customers want to know the shop is professional, responsive and capable before they bring in a vehicle. A website, video presence and consistent social media can help create that confidence.

A website built around leads and credibility

BurkeMedia helped create a website that presented Platinum Auto Repair and Collision in a clearer and more polished way. The site gave customers a place to learn about the business, understand services and take action.

A key part of the website was lead capture. Visitors could submit quote requests and share details that helped the team respond more efficiently. That turned the website into a practical business tool rather than just an online brochure.

Promotional video and original content

Video helped show the professionalism, services and personality of the business. For auto repair and collision work, visuals are especially useful because customers want to see real shop activity, real vehicles and the type of care that goes into the work.

BurkeMedia created promotional video content that could be used on the website, social media and future marketing. These assets gave the business stronger ways to explain what it does and why people should trust the shop.

Social media support from real work

The project also included social media support. Before-and-after photos, project updates and shop visuals can all become useful content when they are organized and presented well.

Ongoing content helps an auto repair business stay visible between customer needs. Someone may not need repairs today, but consistent professional content helps the shop remain familiar when the need comes up.

A more complete digital presence

The combination of website development, lead forms, promotional video and social media gave Platinum Auto Repair and Collision a stronger online foundation. The goal was to help the business look credible, generate inquiries and stay active in front of the community.
