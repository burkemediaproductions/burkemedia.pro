We Launched the New BurkeMedia Productions Website

A quick look at the refreshed BurkeMedia Productions website and how it supports our web design, video, SEO/AIO, content and business systems work in the Coachella Valley.

July 3, 2026 • By Michael Ray Burke • BurkeMedia News

We just launched the refreshed BurkeMedia Productions website, and this one feels especially important because it better represents the way our work has grown.

BurkeMedia has always been about helping people communicate better. Today, that means more than building a good-looking website. It means connecting website design, video production, photography, SEO/AIO, social media, Google Business optimization, branding, apps and business systems into one clearer online presence.

A website that better reflects what BurkeMedia actually does

The new site gives us a stronger home for our services, our local Coachella Valley work, our case studies and the practical problems we help solve for businesses and organizations.

It also gives us a better structure for search and AI discovery. Instead of relying only on broad service pages, we can now build more useful pages around real local needs: Palm Desert web design, Coachella Valley video production, SEO/AIO for small businesses, restaurant websites, church websites, campaign websites and website-to-CRM/business system integrations.

Built around proof, not just promises

One of the main goals of the new BurkeMedia website is to show more of the work behind the work. A case study should not only say that we built a website. It should explain what problem the client had, how the content came together, how video and photography supported the message and how the website can help the business operate better.

That is why we are continuing to add more work examples, articles, local pages and project breakdowns. The strongest SEO and AIO content for BurkeMedia will come from real client work, real local experience and real questions business owners ask us all the time.

What comes next

This launch is the starting point, not the finish line. We will keep improving the site with new city/service pages, better internal links, case studies, local business content, image optimization, Google Business posts and search data from Google Search Console.

For BurkeMedia, the new website is both a portfolio and a living example of what we believe: your website should be useful, searchable, visual, connected and built around what makes your business real.
