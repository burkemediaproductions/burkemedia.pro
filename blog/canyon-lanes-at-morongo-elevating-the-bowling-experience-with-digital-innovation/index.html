Canyon Lanes at Morongo: Elevating the Bowling Experience with Digital Innovation

Website redevelopment, event functionality, video production and social media strategy for Canyon Lanes at Morongo.

August 14, 2024 • By Michael Ray Burke • Featured Client

Canyon Lanes at Morongo is more than just a bowling alley — it is a hub of entertainment and excitement, conveniently located in Cabazon, California. Connected to the renowned Casino Morongo and just a short drive from Indio and the Greater Palm Springs area, Canyon Lanes offers 24 lanes of thrilling bowling experiences, casino gaming machines, an arcade, a full-service Pro Shop and a variety of delicious food options.

Whether you are planning a birthday party, work event or school celebration, Canyon Lanes is the perfect venue.

When Canyon Lanes reopened after the COVID-19 pandemic, they sought to revitalize their digital presence and engage with their audience more effectively. That is where BurkeMedia Productions stepped in, providing a comprehensive suite of digital services to help them reach new heights.

Rebuilding the Website: User-Friendly and Feature-Rich

One of the first tasks was to rebuild the Canyon Lanes website, transforming it into a user-friendly and feature-rich platform. The new website allows users to easily book lanes, reserve the venue for parties and tournaments, and stay updated with a dynamic calendar. We prioritized a seamless experience, making it easy for customers to navigate and for the Canyon Lanes staff to manage.

The result is a website that not only looks great but also functions smoothly, meeting the needs of both users and administrators.

Capturing the Excitement with Dynamic Video Content

To showcase the vibrant atmosphere of Canyon Lanes, we produced a series of promotional videos that truly capture the excitement of the venue. Our video production included dynamic tight shots of bowlers in action and stunning wide shots captured by drones flying inside the alley. These visually engaging videos help potential customers get a feel for the energy and fun that awaits them at Canyon Lanes, making it easier for them to choose this venue for their next event.

Social Media Strategy: Reaching Thousands

When Canyon Lanes reopened after the pandemic, their social media presence needed a fresh start. We consulted with them to develop a strategic social media plan, and the post that announced the re-opening broadened their reach by tens of thousands. By carefully targeting their audience and crafting engaging content, we were able to significantly increase their visibility across social platforms.

This strategic approach ensured that Canyon Lanes could connect with both returning customers and new visitors, driving more foot traffic to the venue.

A Comprehensive Entertainment Experience

Canyon Lanes is not just a place to bowl; it is an entertainment destination that offers something for everyone. With a variety of gaming machines, an exciting arcade and a full-service Pro Shop, visitors can enjoy hours of fun. And when it is time to refuel, the food options at Canyon Lanes are sure to satisfy any craving. Whether you are planning a small gathering or a large event, Canyon Lanes has everything you need to make it a success.
