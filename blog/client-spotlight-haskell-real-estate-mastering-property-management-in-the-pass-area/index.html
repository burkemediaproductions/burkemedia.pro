Client Spotlight: Haskell Real Estate – Mastering Property Management in the Pass Area

A client spotlight on Haskell Real Estate and the website, branding, photography and property management functionality BurkeMedia supported.

BurkeMedia Blog • Featured Client

Haskell Real Estate needed a professional digital presence that could support both real estate and property management needs. The goal was to make the business easier to understand, more polished online and more useful for renters, property owners and potential clients.

Property management websites need to do more than look nice. They have to help people find information, view listings, understand the company and complete practical tasks such as submitting maintenance requests.

A refreshed brand and stronger visual presence

BurkeMedia supported Haskell Real Estate with refreshed branding and a more polished website presentation. The work helped the business feel more modern while still communicating trust, stability and local knowledge.

Photography and video-driven visuals helped the site feel more connected to the actual company instead of relying on generic real estate imagery.

Property management functionality

The website was structured to support rental listings and property management needs. Renters could find helpful information and submit maintenance requests, while the business had a stronger public-facing system for presenting its services.

These kinds of tools help turn a website into something operational. The site does not just explain the business; it supports the work that happens every day.

Clearer information for owners and renters

For property owners, the website needed to communicate experience, professionalism and the value of working with a company that understands the area. For renters, the site needed to make practical information easy to find.

The result was a stronger digital presence for Haskell Real Estate: a website that could represent the company, support property management workflows and create a more professional experience for the people it serves.
