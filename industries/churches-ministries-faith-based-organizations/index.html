Churches, Ministries & Faith-Based Organizations

Websites, livestreams, giving links, calendars, photography, video and ministry content for churches and faith-based organizations.

Industry focus

Digital tools that help churches communicate clearly and welcome people well.

Churches and ministries need websites that serve many people at once: first-time visitors, regular attenders, families, volunteers, staff and people watching from home. BurkeMedia helps organize that information into a warmer, clearer and easier-to-maintain digital presence.

  • Service times, location details and visitor-friendly next steps
  • Livestream sections and video archives for weekly services
  • Giving links connected to existing donation platforms
  • Google Calendar syncing for events, ministries and church office workflows
  • Photography and video that show real church life
  • Ministry pages, event promotion and social media content support
