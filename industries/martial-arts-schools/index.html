Industry focus

A stronger online presence for schools built around trust, training and community.

Martial arts schools need to communicate more than class times. Families want to understand the instructors, the environment, the programs, the discipline and the community. BurkeMedia helps martial arts schools present their programs clearly while creating photo, video, social and SEO/AIO content that feels as strong as the training itself.

  • Class and program pages for adults, kids and specialty training
  • Instructor, team and gym atmosphere content
  • SEO/AIO structure for martial arts and local training searches
  • Event graphics, awards content and promotional media
  • Video reels, training clips and social media storytelling
  • Photography that captures discipline, energy and community
