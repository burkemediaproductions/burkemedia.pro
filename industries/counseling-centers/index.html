Industry Marketing

Counseling Centers & Treatment Organizations

Trust-focused websites, branding, lead capture, video and content for counseling, recovery and support organizations.

Industry focus

A digital presence for sensitive services has to feel clear, compassionate and safe.

Counseling and treatment organizations need websites that communicate professionalism and care while making it easier for people to understand services and reach out. BurkeMedia helps structure content, lead paths, blogs, video and social media in a way that respects the sensitivity of the work.

  • Logo and brand identity for sensitive-service organizations
  • HIPAA-conscious inquiry and lead capture language
  • Service pages that explain care and support clearly
  • Blog structures for education, encouragement and SEO/AIO
  • Promotional and testimonial video production
  • Social media content for awareness, encouragement and visibility
