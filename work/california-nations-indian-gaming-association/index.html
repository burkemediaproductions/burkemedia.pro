Case Study

California Nations Indian Gaming Association / CNIGA

A statewide association website, conference content system, video storytelling and custom Western Indian Gaming Conference mobile app built around the way CNIGA communicates with members, sponsors, attendees and staff.

Visit CNIGA.comWatch the 4K Conference Cut

Project overview

Digital infrastructure for a statewide tribal gaming association and its flagship conference.

For the California Nations Indian Gaming Association, also known as CNIGA, BurkeMedia provided long-term digital, creative and technical support across website development, member resources, database organization, social media content, event coverage and the Western Indian Gaming Conference app experience.

The project needed to do more than present a polished public website. CNIGA needed a dependable place for association updates, member information, conference details, sponsor visibility, video content and staff-managed data to work together without creating extra busywork behind the scenes.

  • Website redevelopment for CNIGA.com
  • Member-resource structure and California Tribe database support
  • Custom backend tools for staff updates and data organization
  • Social media content adapted from association announcements and event coverage
  • Western Indian Gaming Conference video production and 4K recap content
  • Custom WIGC app experience for attendees, sponsors and staff

Website and systems

A professional website that also works as a practical source of truth.

The CNIGA website was rebuilt into a polished, flexible platform for public information, member resources, legislative initiatives, conference updates and staff-managed content.

Website

Public presence

The updated site gave CNIGA a stronger public-facing platform while organizing a large amount of important information for members, partners, media and visitors.

Dashboard

Staff-managed updates

CNIGA staff could publish news, manage resources, share initiatives and keep organization content current without waiting on a developer for every routine change.

Database

Membership organization

The backend helped connect public communication with internal organization, supporting membership information and California Tribe-related data in one cleaner structure.

Western Indian Gaming Conference

A conference app designed for attendees, sponsors and the staff managing everything behind the scenes.

The WIGC app helped conference guests find the right sessions, build their own schedules and receive live updates, while giving sponsors strong visibility and keeping staff updates connected to the website dashboard.

Attendees

Personalized conference schedules

Attendees could browse seminars, events, speakers and conference details, then favorite the sessions and activities most important to them so they did not miss key moments.

Sponsors

Visible sponsor placement

Sponsor logos were placed in highly visible areas throughout the app while staying respectful of the content and the attendee experience.

Staff

One connected dashboard

The app synced with CNIGA’s customized website dashboard, giving staff one source of truth for conference updates instead of forcing the team to maintain disconnected tools.
