Case Study

California Nations Indian Gaming Association / CNIGA

Website redevelopment, member resources, database organization, social content, event coverage and a custom Western Indian Gaming Conference app experience.

Project overview

A long-term digital platform for a statewide association and major conference.

For the California Nations Indian Gaming Association, also known as CNIGA, BurkeMedia provided long-term digital, creative and technical support across website development, member resources, database management, social media content, event coverage and a custom conference app experience for the Western Indian Gaming Conference.

The work combined a public-facing website, practical staff tools, a structured membership database, conference communication, event storytelling and social content that helped CNIGA communicate with members, sponsors, attendees and the broader community.

  • Website redevelopment and member-resource structure
  • Custom backend tools for staff updates and data organization
  • Membership and California Tribe database support
  • Social media content adapted from announcements and email blasts
  • Western Indian Gaming Conference video coverage and event storytelling
  • Custom conference app experience for attendees, sponsors and staff

Website and systems

More than a website: a practical source of truth for the organization.

The CNIGA website was rebuilt into a polished, professional and functional platform that could support public information, member resources, legislative initiatives, industry updates and staff-managed content.

Website

Public presence

The new website gave CNIGA a stronger public-facing platform while organizing a large amount of important information for members, partners and visitors.

Dashboard

Staff-managed updates

CNIGA staff could update members, publish news, manage resources, share legislative initiatives and keep organizational content current without relying on a developer for every change.

Database

Membership organization

The backend helped serve as a source of truth for CNIGA membership information and California Tribe-related data, connecting public communication with internal organization.

Conference and event support

Video coverage, event storytelling and a custom WIGC app experience.

BurkeMedia supported CNIGA’s major annual gatherings, including the Western Indian Gaming Conference, with event coverage and a custom app experience designed around attendees, sponsors and staff.

Attendees

Personalized conference schedules

Attendees could browse seminars, events, speakers and conference details, then favorite the sessions and activities they cared about most.

Sponsors

Visible sponsor placement

Sponsor logos were placed in strategic locations throughout the app, keeping sponsors visible without disrupting the attendee experience.

Staff

One connected dashboard

The app synced with the customized website dashboard so CNIGA staff could manage updates from one source of truth instead of maintaining disconnected systems.
