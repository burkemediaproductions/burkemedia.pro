Project overview

Stronger review presentation and local trust signals for a Greek and Mediterranean restaurant.

Mykonos Greek Mediterranean needed website improvements that made customer reviews, accurate business information and local trust easier to find. The project added a homepage review section, a dedicated Reviews page, stronger navigation to reviews and links back to Google and Yelp profiles.

The work also improved SEO/AIO readiness with better metadata, on-page wording, local restaurant signals, review-related content and consistent hours across the website.

For restaurants, reputation is part of the product. The website should make that reputation easy to see.
