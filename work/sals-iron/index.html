Case Study

Sal’s Iron

Website, photography, video and local visibility support for a skilled trade business built around craftsmanship.

Project overview

A stronger online presence for a company where visual proof matters.

For Sal’s Iron, BurkeMedia helped strengthen the company’s digital presence through website development, photography, video production, social media support and Google Business listing assistance.

For a skilled trade business, visual proof is especially important, so the website and media helped present Sal’s Iron’s craftsmanship in a clearer and more polished way.

  • Website development for services and project presentation
  • Photography showcasing materials, details and finished work
  • Video production highlighting capabilities and process
  • Social media support
  • Google Business listing assistance
  • Local visibility and customer inquiry support
