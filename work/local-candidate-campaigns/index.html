Case Study

Local Candidate & Public Campaigns

Campaign branding, websites, photography, video, social media, print materials and digital tools for local candidates and public campaigns.

Project overview

Specialized campaign support without making politics the whole BurkeMedia story.

BurkeMedia has supported local candidates and public campaigns throughout the Coachella Valley and surrounding areas with full-service creative, digital and strategic campaign support. The work helps candidates present themselves professionally, communicate clearly with voters, build trust, collect support and stay visible throughout the campaign season.

Campaign work has included support for candidates and public servants in Desert Hot Springs, Indio, La Quinta, Rancho Mirage, Cathedral City and Mission Springs Water District races, including work connected to Scott Matas, Gary Gardner, Dirk Voss, Jan Pye, Steve Bronack, Lupe Ramos Amith, Glenn Miller, Deborah McGarrey, Michael O’Keefe, Antonio Baciu and Steve Downs.

  • Campaign logos and visual identities
  • Candidate websites with donation, endorsement, volunteer and contact tools
  • Headshots, photography and video production
  • Endorsement graphics, platform content and campaign updates
  • Mailer, billboard, palm card, business card and sign design
  • Social media strategy, management and digital content creation
