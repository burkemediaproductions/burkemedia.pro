Case Study

Tony Bolivar

A musician website and visual content system built around performances, calendar updates, video and booking opportunities.

Project overview

A professional online home for a working musician.

For Tony Bolivar, BurkeMedia helped support the digital and promotional presence of a musician through website development, event calendar integration, video presentation, photography, headshots, promotional video production and concert coverage.

The website gave fans, venues, event organizers and collaborators a professional place to learn more about Tony’s music and upcoming performances.

  • Musician website development
  • Google Calendar event syncing for upcoming shows
  • Video presentation of performances and work
  • Photography and professional headshots
  • Promotional video production
  • Concert video coverage
