Case Study

Desert Bohemian Trading Post

Branding, website direction and visual content for a quirky vintage resale shop in Twentynine Palms.

Project overview

A desert-inspired identity for a creative resale brand.

For Desert Bohemian Trading Post, BurkeMedia helped support the launch and digital presence of a quirky vintage resale shop in Twentynine Palms through logo design, branding, website work, photography, video production, SEO-minded content, social media copy and brand storytelling.

The branding reflected the shop’s bohemian style, desert personality and eclectic mix of vintage finds, art, furniture, accessories, jewelry, clothing and one-of-a-kind treasures.

  • Logo design and brand identity
  • Website direction and SEO-minded content
  • About page and brand storytelling
  • Photography of inventory and shop personality
  • Video production for the space and products
  • Social media copy for vintage treasures, sustainability and desert creativity
