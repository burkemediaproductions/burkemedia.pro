Case Study

Access Senior Home Care

A warm, polished and lead-focused website for a senior home care company serving families, seniors, caregivers and referral partners.

Project overview

A senior care website built around trust, compassion and practical next steps.

For Access Senior Home Care, BurkeMedia helped create a polished and professional digital presence through a stunning new website, inline video effects, SEO/AIO strategy, Google Business support, lead forms, caregiver recruitment functionality and service-focused content.

The website was designed to feel warm, trustworthy and impressive while still being easy for families, seniors, caregivers and referral partners to navigate.

  • Stunning new website with warm senior care positioning
  • Inline video effects for a modern animated feel
  • SEO/AIO-minded service and location structure
  • Google Business support for local visibility
  • Lead forms for family and client inquiries
  • Caregiver recruitment functionality and service pages
