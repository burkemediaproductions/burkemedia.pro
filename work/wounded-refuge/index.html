Case Study

Wounded Refuge

A warm nonprofit and ministry website built around healing, events, donations, content and community connection.

Project overview

A mission-focused website that helped people understand, support and connect.

For Wounded Refuge, BurkeMedia helped build a stronger digital presence for a nonprofit and ministry-focused organization through a fully branded website, event photography, event video production, SEO, donation functionality, calendar integration, blog structure and organized ministry content.

The design and content were built around the organization’s mission of healing, encouragement, empowerment and community support.

  • Fully branded nonprofit and ministry website
  • SEO-minded mission and ministry content
  • Donation functionality for online giving
  • Google Calendar event syncing
  • Blog structure for updates and resources
  • Event photography and video production

Website tools

A practical digital home for mission, events and support.

The site was built to communicate the organization’s purpose while giving supporters clear ways to connect and give.
