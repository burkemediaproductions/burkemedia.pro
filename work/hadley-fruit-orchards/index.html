Hadley Fruit Orchards

A historic Southern California roadside brand refreshed with ecommerce structure, product content, photography, video, social storytelling and a deeper connection to its 1931 roots.

Project overview

Modern ecommerce, real content and historic storytelling for a beloved roadside destination.

Hadley Fruit Orchards is a Cabazon and San Gorgonio Pass landmark known for dates, dried fruit, nuts, gifts, date shakes and its unmistakable Interstate 10 presence. The project needed to respect the nostalgia people already had for the brand while helping the business present products, cafe offerings, deals and destination information more clearly online.

BurkeMedia helped with the website experience, ecommerce organization, cafe/menu content, product photography, promotional video, social media content and management, and deeper brand storytelling. That included researching historic material and even reaching out to family members connected to the original owners so the site could better honor Paul and Peggy Hadley's story.

A historic destination does not need to become generic online. It needs a digital home that preserves the story while making products, visits and promotions easier to find.

Website + content experience

A brand with real flavor needs more than a product grid.

The refreshed case study shows how product images, social posts, historic photos and motion can work together. Hadley's is not only an ecommerce store. It is a food stop, a road-trip memory, a cafe, a gift destination and a long-running Southern California story.

How the work came together

A connected project, not a one-off website task.

Ecommerce

Products organized for shoppers

Dates, dried fruits, nuts, snacks, gifts and specialty products needed clear categories, strong imagery and better context for people who may be shopping from home or planning a visit.

Story

Legacy with modern tools

The site and content preserved the feeling of a beloved road-trip stop while making room for current deals, cafe content, online shopping and brand history.

Content

Social posts with a reason

Social media management gave Hadley's a way to keep date shakes, products, the building, old photos and seasonal offers in front of customers beyond the website.

Social media content

Turning products, history and daily cravings into scroll-stopping posts.

For Hadley's, social content could lean into the things people already associate with the brand: date shakes, fruit, nuts, historic signage, road trips and the long-running story behind the name.
