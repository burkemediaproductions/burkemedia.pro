Case Study

Vintage Express

A polished digital and brand presence with inventory and ecommerce functionality for Vintage Express.

Project overview

A complete brand and website foundation for products, inventory and customer inquiries.

For Vintage Express, BurkeMedia helped build a complete digital and brand presence through website development, logo design, branding, photography, video production, social media support, Google Business optimization, SEO/AIO strategy, lead forms and inventory/e-commerce functionality.

The project is in the fine-tuning stage, with the major pieces developed and refinements being made to improve presentation, functionality and customer experience.

  • Logo design and brand identity
  • Website development for brand and product presentation
  • Inventory and ecommerce functionality
  • Lead forms for customer questions and buying interest
  • Photography and video production
  • Social media support, Google Business optimization and SEO/AIO strategy
