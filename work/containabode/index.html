Case Study

ContainAbode

A complete digital and brand presence for a modern container-home and alternative housing concept.

Project overview

A modern housing brand built to explain the concept and convert interest.

For ContainAbode, BurkeMedia helped build a complete digital and brand presence for a modern container-home and alternative housing concept through website development, logo design, branding, photography, video production, social media support, Google Business optimization, SEO/AIO strategy, lead forms and property-focused website pages.

The work helped potential customers understand the ContainAbode concept, explore homes and property opportunities, and take the next step toward an inquiry.

  • Logo design and brand identity
  • Website development for container-home concepts and properties
  • Property-focused pages and lead forms
  • Photography and promotional video content
  • SEO/AIO strategy and Google Business support
  • Social media support for awareness and property promotion
