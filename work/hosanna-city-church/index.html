Case Study

Hosanna City Church

Website development, livestream integration, donation support, calendar syncing, photography and video production for a church community.

Project overview

A church website built to help people attend, watch, give and get connected.

For Hosanna City Church, BurkeMedia helped strengthen the church’s digital presence through website development, integrated livestream content, donation support, calendar syncing, photography and video production.

The website was built to make the most important information easy for visitors, members and families to find, including service times, Life Group times, ministry information and key church details.

  • Website development for visitors, members and families
  • Livestream integration for weekly services
  • Donation app connection for online giving
  • Google Calendar syncing for church events
  • Photography for worship, ministries and church life
  • Welcome videos, event promotion videos and ministry-focused video production

Website and integrations

A more connected online home for the church community.

Hosanna City Church needed a website that felt welcoming while also making practical church information easier to manage and easier to find.

Attend

Clear visitor information

Service times, Life Group times, ministry information and next-step details were organized so visitors and members could quickly understand how to connect.

Watch

Livestream connection

The site was synced to display weekly livestreams, helping people stay connected to services even when they could not attend in person.

Give

Donation app support

The website connected to the church’s donation app so members and visitors could give through the church’s existing giving system from the website.

Content and communication

Photography and video that helped the church feel welcoming online.

BurkeMedia captured the people, worship, ministries and community life of Hosanna City Church, then supported the website with video content for visitors, events and ministries.

Calendar

Google Calendar syncing

The website calendar synced with the church office’s Google Calendars, helping event information stay current without duplicate updates.

Photography

Real church visuals

Photography helped the site feel personal, welcoming and authentic by showing the real people and community behind the church.

Video

Welcome and ministry videos

Welcome videos, event promotion videos and ministry-focused videos helped communicate the heart of the church and invite people to participate.
