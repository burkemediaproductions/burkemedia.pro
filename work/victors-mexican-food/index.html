Project overview

A connected restaurant digital strategy built around ordering, menu visibility, food content and local search.

Victor’s Mexican Food needed a stronger digital presence that made the restaurant easier to find, easier to order from and more competitive in local searches for Mexican food in Palm Desert. The work connected website development, local SEO/AIO, Clover-connected ordering, food photography, video production and social media content.

A major focus was creating a better menu experience by keeping Clover as the source of truth while also building a static, crawlable menu structure for search engines and AI platforms.

Restaurant marketing works best when the menu, ordering, photos, videos and local search all support each other.
