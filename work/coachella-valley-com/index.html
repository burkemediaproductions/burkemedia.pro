Local Media Platform Case Study

CoachellaValley.com

Redevelopment, SEO preservation, regional content strategy, events planning, advertising support, and long-term photo and video contributions for a major Coachella Valley digital media platform.

Project focus

Modernizing a regional digital platform without losing the value it had already built.

For CoachellaValley.com, BurkeMedia helped support the redevelopment, modernization, and long-term content strength of a major regional digital media platform serving the Coachella Valley and Palm Springs area.

The work focused on improving the website’s design, structure, usability, and long-term functionality while preserving the value of its existing archive of local articles, community content, and regional search visibility.

  • Website redevelopment and regional platform strategy
  • SEO preservation for a large archive of local content
  • City-specific page planning and local content organization
  • Events calendar, advertising, media kit, and future app planning
  • More than a decade of BurkeMedia video and photography contributions

Regional content strategy

Making the site more useful for locals, visitors, businesses and advertisers.

Because CoachellaValley.com already had a large archive of content and local authority, the redesign strategy needed to organize that value more clearly rather than simply starting over.

Website structure

City-specific content planning

BurkeMedia worked on a stronger structure for regional content, including city-specific page planning, improved local organization, and clearer paths for visitors looking for information about communities throughout the Coachella Valley.

SEO preservation

Protecting existing local authority

The project needed to modernize the experience while preserving the SEO value and search visibility already built through years of local articles, community content, and regional information.

Events

A stronger local events direction

The site direction included a more functional event calendar using The Events Calendar system, with future growth planned around event listings, community submissions, and a broader local events ecosystem.

Platform growth

Directory, advertising and app planning

BurkeMedia also helped plan future expansion into a business directory, advertising opportunities, sponsored content, and a mobile app so CoachellaValley.com could grow beyond a content site into a broader regional platform.

Media and advertising

Helping position CoachellaValley.com as a valuable platform for local visibility.

BurkeMedia supported the brand’s media and advertising strategy, including media kit direction, advertising package ideas, banner ads, sponsored posts, video ads, and ways to present the platform to local businesses and organizations.

The goal was to help the site serve readers while also creating clearer opportunities for advertisers, community partners, businesses, events, and regional organizations looking to reach people in the Coachella Valley and Palm Springs area.

  • Media kit and advertising package direction
  • Sponsored content and banner ad planning
  • Video ad and social media opportunity planning
  • Local business directory growth strategy
  • Future mobile app and community platform vision

Long-term visual content

More than a decade of photo and video contributions across the Coachella Valley.

In addition to website redevelopment and platform strategy, BurkeMedia has served as a video and photo contributor for CoachellaValley.com for over a decade.

This long-term visual content work helped document the people, places, events, businesses, landscapes, and stories that make the Coachella Valley unique. It also strengthened the brand’s social media and content presence with authentic local visuals rather than generic stock-style content.

Content library

Real local visuals for a real regional platform.

BurkeMedia’s photography and video support helped CoachellaValley.com showcase community moments, local events, landmarks, landscapes, businesses, and the energy of the valley through original media.

Project impact

A stronger foundation for a regional media brand.

The work combined redevelopment, SEO preservation, content strategy, city pages, event functionality, advertising planning, directory planning, future app strategy, and ongoing visual storytelling.
