Case Study

Robolights

Website and video storytelling for one of the Coachella Valley’s most visually memorable art attractions.

Project overview

A digital presence and video archive for a uniquely visual holiday art installation.

For Robolights, BurkeMedia helped support one of the Coachella Valley’s most unique and visually memorable attractions through website development, annual video coverage and video tours of the holiday light display and art installation.

Because Robolights was such a visual and unconventional attraction, the website and videos needed to communicate the scale, creativity and personality of the installation.

  • Website development for the attraction
  • Annual video coverage during the holiday light display
  • Video tours of the art installation
  • Visual storytelling around scale, color and creativity
  • Content that helped people experience the display online
  • Promotional and preservation-focused media assets

Website and video

Helping people discover, experience and remember the installation.

The work gave Robolights a dedicated online presence and professional video assets that captured its unforgettable character.
