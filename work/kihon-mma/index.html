Martial Arts & Fitness Case Study

Kihon MMA

A highly branded website, search-focused content strategy, video, social media, event graphics and photography for a martial arts school built around discipline, energy and community.

Project focus

A digital presence built to feel as disciplined, polished and community-driven as the gym itself.

For Kihon MMA, BurkeMedia helped strengthen the brand’s digital presence through website development, SEO/AIO strategy, video production, social media content, graphic design, event promotion and photography.

The website is a major part of the project. It was built to be visually strong, highly on brand and useful as both a marketing tool and an informational resource for students, families and prospective members.

  • Industry: Martial Arts & Fitness Studios
  • Website development and branded page structure for classes, programs, instructors and the gym environment.
  • SEO/AIO strategy to support visibility for martial arts, MMA, Muay Thai and youth training searches.
  • Video, social media, event graphics, poster design and awards banquet photography.

Website + search visibility

A site designed to look professional, rank well and answer the questions families and students actually ask.

The Kihon MMA website presents the gym with a polished, professional look while making it easy for visitors to learn about classes, programs, instructors and the training environment. Its structure, content and local SEO/AIO approach help support discovery through Google and AI-driven search while reinforcing the credibility of the brand.

Website

Highly branded presentation

The site was designed to feel visually aligned with Kihon MMA’s identity, giving the gym a stronger first impression for new students and families.

SEO/AIO

Built for local discovery

The content supports search visibility for martial arts, MMA, Muay Thai, youth training and related local searches.

Information

Clear paths for visitors

Visitors can more easily understand classes, programs, instructors and what to expect when they walk into the gym.

Content creation

Video, graphics and photography that show the energy of the gym.

BurkeMedia has produced video content for Kihon MMA to showcase the energy, discipline and community of the gym. These videos help highlight training sessions, students, instructors, classes and the overall atmosphere that makes Kihon MMA unique.

We have also designed posters and social media graphics for event announcements, helping promote upcoming events in a clean, branded and professional way across digital and printed formats.

  • Social media video content for classes, training and gym atmosphere.
  • Event announcement graphics and branded poster design.
  • Photography for the annual Awards Banquet, including award recipients, community celebration and meaningful moments.
  • Content that helps Kihon MMA stay visible, active and professional across its website and social platforms.

Project impact

Helping the online brand match the real-world training environment.

The goal was to help Kihon MMA look as professional, disciplined and community-driven online as it is in person.

Brand trust

A stronger first impression

The website and visuals help prospective students and families quickly understand the quality and seriousness of the gym.

Content library

Ongoing marketing assets

Video, photography and graphics give Kihon MMA reusable content for social media, event promotion and future campaigns.

Community

More than classes

Awards banquet photography and training content help document the people, progress and community that make the gym special.
