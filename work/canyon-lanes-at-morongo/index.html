Project overview

A more useful and exciting digital presence for a bowling and entertainment venue.

Canyon Lanes at Morongo needed a website and content presence that could support customers and staff. The website was redeveloped to make it easier for guests to book lanes, inquire about parties, book events, learn about tournaments and view calendar information.

The project also included promotional video work and social media strategy around the venue’s reopening period after COVID, helping the venue stay visible and engaging with the local community.

For an entertainment venue, the website should make people want to go there and make it easy to plan the visit.

A deeper look at the Canyon Lanes digital refresh.

The original Canyon Lanes article expands on how BurkeMedia helped the venue improve its website, content presence and reopening visibility.

Canyon Lanes at Morongo is more than a bowling alley. It is an entertainment venue connected to Casino Morongo, with bowling lanes, gaming machines, arcade options, a Pro Shop, food and event-friendly experiences for birthdays, work gatherings, school celebrations and tournaments.

When Canyon Lanes reopened after the COVID-19 pandemic, BurkeMedia supported a refreshed digital presence that made the venue easier to understand, easier to promote and easier for customers to engage with online.

The website work focused on useful customer paths: lane booking, party and venue reservations, tournament information and calendar updates. The article also highlights promotional video production and a social media strategy that helped broaden awareness during a key reopening period.
