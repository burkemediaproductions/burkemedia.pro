Client Work

Direct Connect Enterprise

Logo design, full website development, photography, promotional video production, founder interview storytelling, video-based social media content and ongoing social media management for a mission-driven organization.

Project focus

A complete digital presence built around mission, story and support.

For Direct Connect Enterprise, BurkeMedia helped build a complete digital presence for a mission-driven organization through logo design, full website development, photography, promotional video production, interview storytelling, months’ worth of video-based social media content, and ongoing social media management.

The work helped the organization communicate its mission around disability awareness, MS, encouragement, community support and artist empowerment in a way that felt professional, human and easy to understand.

  • Industry: Healthcare Education, Disability Awareness & Artist Support
  • Logo design and brand identity for a mission-centered organization.
  • Full website development with awareness, storytelling and shop support.
  • Founder interview storytelling built around Chris Enrico’s lived experience with MS.
  • Photography, promotional video, video-based social content and ongoing social media management.

Brand and website

Giving the organization a clear visual foundation and central online home.

The project included creating the Direct Connect Enterprise logo and brand identity, giving the organization a professional and recognizable visual foundation. The branding needed to support a mission centered around disability awareness, MS, encouragement, community support and artist empowerment, so the identity was designed to feel meaningful, human and mission-focused.

Brand Identity

A mission-focused logo and visual direction

BurkeMedia helped turn the concept into a recognizable brand that could support the website, social media, video content, shop experience and future outreach.

Website Development

A full website built around awareness and action

BurkeMedia built the full Direct Connect Enterprise website, creating a central place for people to learn about the organization, understand its mission, connect with its story and engage with its work.

Founder interview

Helping Chris Enrico share the heart behind Direct Connect Enterprise.

A major part of the work centered on capturing the story of Chris Enrico, founder of Direct Connect Enterprise. Through interview-based video content, BurkeMedia helped communicate Chris’s personal experience with MS, his journey learning to live with disability, and his desire to encourage and support others facing similar challenges.

Interview Storytelling

Personal, mission-centered video

The promotional video work was designed to explain the heart behind Direct Connect Enterprise in a personal and meaningful way. Viewers could hear directly from Chris and understand the mission through his story, perspective and lived experience.

Photography

Visuals that made the mission feel real

The project also included photography to support the website, social media and broader marketing materials, helping the brand feel more authentic, personal and connected to the real people and purpose behind the organization.

Content system

Turning one meaningful story into months of video-based content.

BurkeMedia produced months’ worth of video-based social media content for Direct Connect Enterprise. Interview footage and promotional content were repurposed into reels, shorts and other social media pieces designed to educate, encourage and reach people across platforms.

Short-Form Video

Reels, Shorts and social clips

Interview and promotional footage was structured for multiple uses, including short-form reels, TikToks, YouTube Shorts and longer-form video content.

Social Management

Ongoing visibility and consistency

In addition to producing the content, BurkeMedia provided ongoing social media management to help keep the organization visible, consistent and connected to its audience through regular mission-driven content.

Mission Messaging

Encouragement across platforms

The content was planned to help Direct Connect Enterprise educate, encourage and communicate its purpose consistently across the website, social media and video channels.

Digital messaging

Disability awareness, community support and artist empowerment.

Direct Connect Enterprise also included a website and shop component connected to art created by artists with MS and other disabilities. The online shop helped create a way for people to support disabled artists directly by purchasing their work, tying the organization’s digital presence to both awareness and practical support.

Awareness

MS and disability-focused messaging

The messaging helped communicate Chris’s journey, the realities of living with disability, and the desire to encourage others through honest, human storytelling.

Shop Support

Artists with MS and disabilities

The online shop direction connected the mission to a practical way for people to support disabled artists by purchasing and sharing their work.

Careful Language

Thoughtful health-related wording

The messaging also included careful storytelling around resources and experiences that had been beneficial to Chris, including Regenerative Cells, while avoiding terminology that could create confusion or compliance concerns.

Project impact

A professional platform that can grow with the organization.

Overall, the work for Direct Connect Enterprise combined logo design, full website development, photography, promotional video production, founder storytelling, months of video-based social media content, ongoing social media management, disability awareness messaging, and support for an online art shop benefiting artists with MS and other disabilities.

The goal was to help the organization share its mission clearly, humanize its story, support artists, encourage others, and create a professional platform that could grow with the organization.

  • Online shop messaging connected to artist support
