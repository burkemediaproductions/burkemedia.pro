Counseling & Treatment Case Study

Change The Influence

A compassionate, professional digital presence for a Riverside counseling and addiction treatment organization, built around trust, privacy, emotional storytelling and clear paths to reach out.

Project focus

A brand, website and content system built for sensitive conversations.

For Change The Influence, BurkeMedia helped build a professional, emotional and conversion-focused online presence for a Riverside-based counseling and addiction treatment organization. The work combined branding, website development, HIPAA-conscious lead capture, video production, photography and months of social media content.

The goal was to create a trustworthy digital experience that helped people understand the organization, feel safe reaching out and take a first step toward support and healing.

  • Industry: Counseling, addiction treatment and behavioral health services.
  • Services: logo design, brand identity, website development, video production, photography, blog structure and social media content.
  • Website focus: service organization, privacy-conscious inquiry paths and clear calls to action.
  • Content focus: emotional promotional video, testimonial videos, educational content and ongoing awareness.

Brand identity

A visual foundation for healing, transformation and trust.

The Change The Influence name carries a powerful message: helping people redirect life toward something healthier and better. The brand needed to feel meaningful, supportive and professional without losing the emotional weight of the work.

Logo & identity

A clearer public-facing brand

BurkeMedia created the logo and visual identity for Change The Influence, giving the organization a consistent foundation for the website, marketing materials, social media and community communication.

Messaging

Supportive, serious and hopeful

The brand direction was built to communicate compassion, professionalism and the possibility of change, helping the organization speak to people and families looking for guidance, treatment or support.

Website development

A site designed to organize services and help people take the next step.

The Change The Influence website was developed to clearly explain the organization’s services and make it easier for potential clients or family members to reach out. Services were organized in a clean, understandable way so visitors could quickly learn what support was available and how the organization could help.

The website also included a blog structure, giving Change The Influence a place to publish helpful articles, educational content, updates and resources. That helped support both visitors and search visibility while giving the organization more ways to speak to people looking for answers.

  • Clean service organization for counseling and addiction treatment support.
  • Lead capture built with privacy and sensitivity in mind.
  • Blog structure for educational content, resources and search visibility.
  • Website content designed to feel compassionate, professional and easy to understand.

Video & testimonials

Emotional storytelling that helped communicate the mission.

BurkeMedia produced a strong promotional video for Change The Influence that was designed to communicate the heart of the organization, the seriousness of the work and the hope behind the services.

Promotional video

A story-driven introduction

The promotional video gave prospective clients and families a clearer sense of the mission and the people behind the organization, helping the digital presence feel more human and emotionally grounded.

Testimonial videos

Real voices and trust signals

BurkeMedia also captured testimonial videos for the website, giving real stories a place in the online experience and helping build credibility, trust and emotional connection.

Social content

Months of content to support awareness and encouragement.

Beyond the website and videos, BurkeMedia created months’ worth of social media content for Change The Influence. The content supported awareness, education, encouragement and ongoing visibility for the organization.

This helped the brand stay active online while giving the organization consistent, mission-aligned content to share with people in the community.

  • Awareness-focused social posts.
  • Educational and supportive messaging.
  • Content designed around counseling, recovery and encouragement.
  • Reusable assets built from the brand, website, photos and videos.

Project impact

A professional online presence for a sensitive, trust-based organization.

The finished digital presence helped Change The Influence look more established, organized and compassionate online while giving visitors clearer ways to understand services, view testimonials, read helpful content and submit inquiries.

Trust

The website and branding helped the organization feel credible while still communicating care, hope and emotional support.

Conversion

Content
