Auto Repair Case Study

Platinum Auto Repair & Collision

Website development, quote-request lead generation, promotional video production, content creation and ongoing social media management for an auto repair and collision shop.

Project overview

A website and content system built to turn auto repair interest into real follow-up.

For Platinum Auto Repair & Collision, BurkeMedia helped build a stronger digital presence through website development, lead-generation functionality, promotional video production, content creation and ongoing social media management.

The goal was to make the shop easier to trust online, easier to contact and easier to keep visible with consistent content that shows real repair and collision work.

  • Industry: Auto Repair & Collision
  • Website development with quote-request lead generation.
  • Team notification workflow for faster follow-up.
  • Promotional video production and original content capture.
  • Ongoing social media management using real before-and-after repair photos.

Website + lead generation

More than a digital brochure.

The website was developed to help visitors become leads by requesting quotes directly online. When customers submit quote requests, the Platinum team is notified so they can respond quickly and move the conversation forward.

Quote Requests

Customers can take action online

The site gives potential customers a clear path to request a quote instead of only browsing services and leaving without taking the next step.

Follow-Up

The team gets notified

Quote submissions are designed to reach the team so the shop can respond quickly and turn website traffic into actionable opportunities.

Trust

The site supports credibility

The website, visuals and messaging work together to present Platinum as professional, responsive and capable.

Video + content

Showing the shop, the services and the quality of the work.

BurkeMedia produced promotional video content for Platinum Auto Repair & Collision to showcase the business, its services and the quality of its work. The video content helped give potential customers a clearer sense of the shop’s professionalism, capabilities and trustworthiness.

Platinum was also one of the clients BurkeMedia shot content for as part of a broader social media and marketing strategy, creating months’ worth of content to keep the business visible, active and consistent online.

  • Promotional video production for the shop.
  • Original content capture for website and social use.
  • Service-focused content to explain what the shop does.
  • Brand visuals that help the business feel more professional online.
  • Reusable content assets for ongoing marketing.

Ongoing social media

Turning real repair work into consistent marketing.

BurkeMedia continues to manage Platinum Auto Repair & Collision’s social media presence. As the team sends before-and-after photos of repair and collision work, BurkeMedia turns those assets into polished social media content that helps tell the story of the work, show real results and keep the brand active in front of potential customers.

Before + After

Real work becomes proof

Repair photos can show visible transformation, helping customers understand the quality and value of the shop’s work.

Consistency

The brand stays active

Ongoing social content helps the business remain present online between larger campaigns, videos or website updates.

Local Trust

Content supports reputation

Professional, consistent posts help the shop look current, responsive and reliable when potential customers research repair options.

Project impact

A connected digital presence for leads, trust and visibility.

Overall, the work for Platinum Auto Repair & Collision combines website development, quote-request lead generation, team notification workflows, promotional video production, original content creation and ongoing social media management.
