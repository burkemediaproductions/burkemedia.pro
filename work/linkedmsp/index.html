Case Study

LinkedMSP

Website redesign, WordPress development, service organization and scalable backend planning for an established managed service provider.

Project overview

A cleaner, more organized digital presence for an established IT services company.

For LinkedMSP, BurkeMedia helped modernize and restructure the digital presence of an established managed service provider through website redesign, WordPress development, service page organization, SEO-conscious content structure, accessibility improvements and custom backend planning.

LinkedMSP already had established branding, service offerings, content, blog material, market positioning, SEO history and a clear business identity. The work focused on taking those existing strengths and turning them into a cleaner, more professional website experience with video backgrounds and a smooth, on-brand content flow.

  • Website redesign for an established managed service provider
  • Custom WordPress development with ACF-powered structures
  • Service groups, service pages and supporting factoids organized for clarity
  • SEO-conscious structure, headings and content organization
  • Accessibility improvements and keyboard-friendly navigation planning
  • Reusable structures that helped inform BurkeMedia’s broader ServiceUp framework

Website structure

Service pages and content systems built for clarity.
