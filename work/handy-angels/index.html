Project overview

More than a website: a public-facing brand and an internal service workflow.

Handy Angels needed a digital presence that could quickly communicate what the company does, who is behind it and why homeowners, rentals and property managers can trust the team with urgent repairs and ongoing property care.

BurkeMedia helped shape the project across the full customer journey: logo and visual identity, website structure, service pages, real photography, inline video, printed brand pieces and a service request path that sends new requests into a custom app on the client's phone. From there, the request can be reviewed and shared with the right team member instead of getting buried in scattered texts or missed calls.

For a home-service business, the website should not only look credible. It should help the work move.

Brand identity

A friendly, memorable identity that works online, on vehicles and in the field.

The Handy Angels brand needed to feel helpful without looking generic. The logo, blue-and-gold color direction and guardian-angel idea gave the business a visual system that can live on the website, shirts, cards, magnets, service vehicles and social content.

The line “Treating your home like it’s our own” became a useful trust signal because it connects the brand promise to what customers actually care about: response, care, communication and respect for their property.

Website experience

Inline video makes the page feel alive without getting in the customer's way.

The Handy Angels site uses motion and real visuals to add energy while keeping the request path clear. The video helps the brand feel active, local and service-ready, while the page copy keeps customers moving toward the next step.

Customer path

Clear request flow

Service requests are designed to move from the website into a custom mobile workflow so the client can review the need, respond quickly and route it to the right person.

Content strategy

Real trust signals

Team photos, service language, reviews, emergency messaging and local-area content help the site feel grounded in real people and real Coachella Valley service needs.

Local SEO/AIO

Pages with purpose

The service content supports search and AI-assisted discovery without sounding like placeholder text or keyword stuffing.

Printed and physical brand pieces

The brand carries beyond the screen.

For service businesses, customers see the brand in multiple places: a card, a magnet, a shirt, a truck, a social post and the website. We kept those pieces connected so Handy Angels feels consistent wherever someone encounters it.

People-first content

Real photos help a service brand feel more trustworthy.

Instead of leaning only on stock images, the project uses team and partner portraits to make the business feel personal, local and approachable.
