Project overview

A warmer, more credible digital presence for a local service business built around trust, care and real content.

Handy Angels needed an online presence that felt polished, caring and dependable. The project combined brand identity, website development, photography, video production, content strategy and local SEO/AIO improvements so potential customers could understand the company and feel comfortable reaching out.

Instead of relying on generic service-business imagery, the site was shaped around real people, real visuals and clear service messaging. The result is a digital foundation that better communicates trust, reliability and helpfulness.

A service business website has to do more than list tasks. It has to make people feel safe choosing the company.
