Case Study

The Law Offices of Quintanilla & Associates

Digital and promotional support for a law firm’s professional, credible and community-facing presence.

Project overview

Professional legal marketing support built around trust and visibility.

For The Law Offices of Quintanilla & Associates, also known as QA Lawyers, BurkeMedia helped support the firm’s professional public presence through website-related work, digital marketing support, promotional photography, promotional video production and seasonal advertising content.

As a legal services business, trust and professionalism are especially important, so the digital and visual materials needed to communicate experience, reliability and accessibility.

  • Website-related and digital marketing support
  • Promotional photography for public-facing materials
  • Promotional video production
  • Community-facing seasonal advertising content
  • Christmas promotional videos aired in local movie theaters
  • Professional visuals designed to support client trust
